# Terms of Service — Smart Publisher Closed Beta

Effective date: 2026-07-30

Smart Publisher is available only to invited closed-beta testers.  By using
it, a tester agrees to use only organisations, Pages, channels, content, and
credentials they are authorised to manage.

The beta currently supports publishing to Telegram and Facebook Pages only.
Instagram, WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn, and other visible integrations are marked
`Coming soon` and must not be represented as available publishing channels.
Facebook Page access for external testers remains subject to Meta App Review
and real staging evidence; mentioning the Page-only path here is not a claim
that those external gates have been completed.

Testers remain responsible for the legality, accuracy, rights, and platform
policy compliance of their content.  Publishing can fail because a provider
rejects a token, Page, channel, permission, rate, or content.  The system
records the outcome and may retry an eligible failure; it does not guarantee
delivery or make a mock response look like a real post.

Do not share access tokens, bot tokens, passwords, APK signing material, or
provider secrets.  Report a suspected credential exposure to the deployment
operator immediately so it can be rotated.

The deployment operator may suspend the beta or revoke access to protect
users, providers, and data.  Any release rollback depends on a real,
hosting-specific procedure maintained by that operator; this app has no
automated rollback control.  A public operator identity, support contact, and
governing-law terms still need to be supplied by the operator before a public
release; this closed-beta document does not invent those facts.
